# HurricanesAndGasPrices

**Project Description:**
How does the presence of a hurricane in the Atlantic Ocean impact Florida gas prices? In this project we aim to create a predictive model that can utilize indicators of a hurricane’s progression to predict the best time to start preparing for the hurricane as indicated by gas prices. Predicting gas prices can give insights into other changes in the economy that can take place when a natural disaster is imminent. In order to implement a comparison of impacts due to variation in population, we will examine data from the Florida peninsula (Broward County) and panhandle (Escambia County) for data. We plan to utilize comparison analysis between counties and decision trees.

A thorough understanding of the impact of hurricanes and large scale natural weather disasters is imperative for the wellbeing of the citizens of Florida and the southern economies. This understanding would benefit Floridians by providing insight into how to best prepare for extreme weather events based on the phases of a developing hurricane.
